Program Overview

In this digital age when children are inclined towards screens, our program gives the option of socializing and learning to read through play. Let's Read takes young learners on an exciting journey from beginner to proficient readers through engaging games and playful activities. Our books and games are designed to boost confidence and self-esteem, fostering a love for reading from an early age. The hands-on nature of our program encourages physical movement, communication, and collaboration, providing a unique learning experience that goes beyond screens.

Empowering Learners

Our program is designed for English second-language learners, focusing on listening and speaking skills before reading. The playful material allows children to:

  • Make mistakes without guilt
  • Exercise personal choice
  • Repeat until mastered

This structured approach nurtures confidence and enhances higher-order thinking skills.

Why Choose Let's Read?

While many schools in India rely on foreign-designed reading programs, Let's Read stands out as a well-researched, culturally relevant program tailored specifically for the Indian context.

Culturally Relevant

Relatable words and pictures ensure a smooth transition from the mother tongue to English.

Proven Results

Effective in both low-income and affluent schools across diverse settings.

Structured Methods

Specially designed for teaching English to non-native speakers effectively.

Balanced Learning

Offers a hands-on approach as an alternative to digital learning.

Through fun activities, we address the challenges of the digital age, empowering educators, parents, and caregivers to support early literacy development. Our hands-on approach offers a balanced alternative to digital learning, nurturing holistic growth in children.

Salient Features

Holistic Approach

Seamlessly integrates play-based reading, interactive experiences, and captivating learning materials to accommodate diverse learning preferences.

Play Phonics

Develops expertise in letter-sound relationships, decoding, and word construction through an engaging kinesthetic phonics curriculum.

Cognitive Stimulation

Activities and games foster higher-order thinking skills, ensuring steady progress in reading proficiency.

Preschool Literacy Audit

Assists schools in implementing effective foundational literacy strategies to boost literacy rates and ensure smooth transitions in reading books in higher classes.

Reading Age Tool

Tracks each child's reading age, enabling schools to monitor and improve literacy standards over time.

Parental Engagement

Equips parents with resources and guidance to actively participate in their child's literacy journey.

Equity in Education

Let's Read promotes equity in education by serving both low-income and affluent schools, with activities tailored to meet the diverse needs of all children.

Our kinesthetic activities also support the literacy learning of neurodivergent children, fostering self-correction and emotional development.

Learning Through Fun and Friendship

Research shows that children are naturally drawn towards play. Our activities are carefully curated to progress through different stages of play:

Solitary Play

Activity is done individually by the child, allowing them to explore and learn at their own pace while building confidence and independence.

Collaborative Play

A group of children play a game together, developing social skills, teamwork, and communication while learning through shared experiences.

These activities make reading and learning fun and enjoyable, reducing the stress of making mistakes and the boredom of reading from textbooks.

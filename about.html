Program Overview

In this digital age when children are inclined towards screens, our program gives the option of socializing and learning to read through play. Let's Read takes young learners on an exciting journey from beginner to proficient readers through engaging games and playful activities. Our books and games are designed to boost confidence and self-esteem, fostering a love for reading from an early age. The hands-on nature of our program encourages physical movement, communication, and collaboration, providing a unique learning experience that goes beyond screens.

Empowering Learners

Let's Read is designed to empower children who learn English as a second language. We begin with activities that support listening and speaking before we teach reading. The playful nature of the reading material allows children to:

  • Make mistakes without feeling guilty
  • Exercise personal choice and independence
  • Repeat activities until mastered

Our structured and progressive approach nurtures confidence, self-worth, and self-esteem in every child. As children grow more confident, the program provides just the right amount of cognitive stimulation and enhances higher-order thinking ability.

Equity in Education

Let's Read promotes equity in education by serving both low-income and affluent schools, with activities tailored to meet the diverse needs of all children.

Our kinesthetic activities also support the literacy learning of neurodivergent children, fostering self-correction and emotional development.

Learning Through Fun and Friendship

Research shows that children are naturally drawn towards play. Our activities are carefully curated to progress through different stages of play:

Solitary Play

Activity is done individually by the child, allowing them to explore and learn at their own pace while building confidence and independence.

Collaborative Play

A group of children play a game together, developing social skills, teamwork, and communication while learning through shared experiences.

These activities make reading and learning fun and enjoyable, reducing the stress of making mistakes and the boredom of reading from textbooks.

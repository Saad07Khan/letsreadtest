IMPLEMENTATION TIMELINE

Simple plan — big impact.

A structured 12-week journey from baseline assessment to measurable outcomes

WEEKS 0–2

Baseline & Orientation

Diagnostic assessment or quick check establishes starting points, while teacher orientation ensures everyone is equipped and ready.

Daily Routines & Rotations

Consistent 10–15 minute daily routines combined with small-group rotations build momentum and engagement.

WEEKS 3–10
WEEKS 6–8

Mid-Cycle Check & Regroup

A pulse check helps identify progress and challenges, allowing for timely adjustments and regrouping strategies.

Follow-up & Parent Summary

Final assessment captures growth and outcomes, with summaries shared with parents to celebrate progress.

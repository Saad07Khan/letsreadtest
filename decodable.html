Tips for Teachers

  • 1. Read the book aloud first to model fluency, then ask learners to whisper-read.
  • 2. Highlight target sound patterns before reading and practise them in isolation.
  • 3. Use picture cues to build meaning and keep comprehension questions simple.
  • 4. Encourage confident reading — praise accuracy and strategy use rather than speed.

HOW TO USE AT HOME

A Simple 5-Step Routine

Just 5-10 minutes a day to build confident readers

1

Warm-up (1 min)

Review 5 target sounds/words.

2

Finger Track (2–3 min)

Child reads while pointing to each word.

3

Reread (1–2 min)

Same page; focus on smooth phrasing.

4

Talk Time (1–2 min)

Discuss the story with 'why/how' questions.

5

Celebrate! (10 sec)

Add a sticker to the tracker and celebrate!

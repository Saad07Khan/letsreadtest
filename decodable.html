HOW TO USE AT HOME

A simple 5-step routine

1

Warm-up (1 min)

Review 5 target sounds/words.

2

Finger track (2–3 min)

Child reads; point to each word.

3

Reread (1–2 min)

Same page; focus on phrasing.

4

Talk time (1–2 min)

Answer 1 'why/how' using the picture.

5

Celebrate (10 sec)

Sticker on tracker; pick tomorrow's page.

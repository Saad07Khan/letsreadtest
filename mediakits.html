Official Description (Boilerplate)

Let's Read India builds kinesthetic phonics kits, decodable readers, and a 5–7 minute reading-age diagnostic so homes and classrooms can teach reading in 10–15 minutes a day. Aligned to NEP/NCF, our materials are written for Indian L1/L2 learners, screen-free, and designed for fast, visible progress. We partner with schools, NGOs, and CSR teams across India.
